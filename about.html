We are Campus Circle

Bridging the gap between academic success and student life.

Our Story

Campus Circle started with a simple observation: University life is fragmented. Students struggle to find reliable lecture notes, study partners are hard to coordinate, and buying textbooks or selling old gear often leads to scams or unsafe meetups.

We decided to fix this by building a centralized digital campus. What began as a small project to share past questions has grown into a comprehensive ecosystem where verified students can ace their exams and trade safely—all in one place.

🚀 Our Mission

To empower every university student by democratizing access to academic resources and creating a safe, trust-based economy within campuses.

🌍 Our Vision

To become the student operating system—the first place you go to study, connect, buy, or sell, knowing you are interacting with verified peers.
