About Us

Campus Circle

Your Ultimate Academic Success Partner

Campus Circle is a platform where students can access lecture materials and tools aimed at improving their academic journey. We are focused on empowering students with accessible resources and technology for academic excellence.

Why is Campus Circle safe? It is verified and aimed at bringing innovation to student learning, building them academically and ensuring they have exactly what they need to pass.

We are digitizing the campus library experience so you can focus on what actually matters: studying smarter, passing your exams, and achieving academic excellence.

Key Features

Complete Lecture Notes

We know not everyone can write fast enough to copy everything during a lecture. We give you access to comprehensive, well-structured notes so you never miss out.

The Digital Library

Instantly unlock and read softcopy PDFs of your faculty and departmental handouts, textbooks, and past questions right from your phone.

Hardcopy Delivery

Prefer reading on physical paper? No problem. Order printed materials directly through the app and get them delivered to your hostel or the campus gate.

Instant Alerts

Get real-time notifications on your orders and be the first to know when new study materials for your specific department are uploaded.
